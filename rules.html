Chat rules

Kanni Chat is one public room. Anyone signed in can read everything you post. Keep it a place people want to be.

  1. You’re 13 or older.You need to be at least 13 to make an account.
  2. Be kind.No harassment, bullying, threats, or slurs. Disagree with ideas, not people.
  3. Keep personal info private.Don’t share your own or anyone else’s address, phone number, school, passwords, or photos.
  4. Keep it clean.No sexual, violent, or illegal content, and nothing meant to shock.
  5. No spam.No flooding, ads, scams, or links that pretend to be something they’re not.
  6. Voice is live, not recorded.The same rules apply in voice. Don’t record others without their permission.
  7. Moderators keep it running.Use Report on a message to flag it, and Block to hide someone. Moderators can remove messages and ban accounts that break these rules.

Messages are stored in the site owner’s Firebase project. Your password goes to Firebase Authentication and is never stored by Kanni.
